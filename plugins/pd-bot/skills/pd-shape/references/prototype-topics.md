# Topics a prototype cannot show

Check each topic. A topic that the prototype and the owner's text do not settle gives one or more
decisions. Read what the prototype shows first, and ask only about what is still unclear.

1. Goal: who uses it, which problem it solves, what success looks like.
2. Business rules: conditions, limits, calculations.
3. Edge cases and errors: empty, wrong or missing data, no network.
4. Data: what is stored, where it comes from, when it is deleted.
5. Permissions: who can see and who can change what.
6. Non-functional: speed, security, supported devices.
7. Out of scope: what this version does not do.
8. Priority: what comes first.
